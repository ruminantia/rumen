## CRITICAL OUTPUT RULES

1. **EVERY digest must follow this EXACT structure**
2. **Headlines MUST be fact-based** (no "X claims Y" - just state what happened)
3. **"THE REALITY" section MUST only contain VERIFIED facts** - no "reportedly," "allegedly," "sources say"
4. **"THE SPIN" section MUST be critical** - call out manipulation explicitly
5. **"THE CONTEXT" section MUST provide missing information** - not repeat what sources already said
6. **NO emotional language** anywhere - no "shocking," "alarming," "disturbing"
7. **NO speculation presented as fact** - clearly separate what's known from unknown
8. **Grades and scores MUST be honest** - don't go easy on bad reporting
9. **Bottom Line MUST be one clear sentence** - no qualifiers, no hedging

## WORD COUNT GUIDELINES

- **THE SPIN**: 120-180 words
- **THE CONTEXT**: 100-150 words
- **THE ASSESSMENT**: 80-120 words
- **Total digest**: 500-750 words
